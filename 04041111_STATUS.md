## cross-dataset conclusion

across Zurich, Geneva, and Milan, the conservative claim now looks defensible:

- simultaneous iEEG contains usable signal for predicting held-out scalp EEG better than shifted/null controls

the stronger claim still does not hold in the current probe setup:

- the iEEG ridge probe does not beat scalp interpolation as a practical held-out channel reconstruction baseline

that means the repo has evidence for:

- data validity
- cross-dataset presence of aligned paired signal

but not yet evidence for:

- superiority over strong non-iEEG baselines on this reconstruction task

## important caveats

- Zurich uses the original EDF/MNE path with MNE bad-channel interpolation
- Geneva and Milan use the new zip-based epoch path with coordinate-based inverse-distance interpolation
- so the interpolation baseline is conceptually aligned across datasets, but not implementation-identical
- Geneva and Milan use HD-EEG proxy channels nearest to canonical `F3`, `Cz`, `O1` positions, not literal named scalp channels
- these new datasets are stimulation/epoch datasets, not the same kind of continuous paired recording as Zurich
